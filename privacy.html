Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Spam Email Detection System uses Gmail information.

Gmail Access

The application requests permission to read Gmail emails only for the purpose of detecting spam emails.

Use of Information

Gmail email information is used only for spam detection. The application does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Data Security

Users can stop Gmail access at any time by removing the application's permission from their Google Account.
